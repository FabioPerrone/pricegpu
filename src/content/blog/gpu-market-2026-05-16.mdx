SAN FRANCISCO, MAY 16, 2026 — Vast.ai retains its title as the cheapest provider for the h100-80gb-sxm, now priced at $2.29/hr. 

The stability of high-end GPU prices is a significant development, driven by the ongoing balance between supply and demand in the cloud market. 

According to PriceGPU data, engineers training large models on high-end GPUs like the h100-80gb-sxm and a100-80gb-sxm are getting the best deals from Vast.ai and Fluidstack, respectively, with the former charging $2.29/hr and the latter charging $1.85/hr for the a100-80gb-sxm, while Vast.ai charges $1.89/hr for the h100-80gb-pcie. 

PriceGPU tracking shows that prices have not changed significantly since the last scrape, with the rtx-4090 remaining at $0.24/hr on Salad, and the a10g staying at $1.10/hr on Modal, indicating a period of market stability that allows engineers to plan their budgets with more certainty. 

The lack of significant price changes means that engineers can reliably budget for their GPU needs without expecting sudden spikes or drops in costs. 

Engineers deciding what to rent today should consider Vast.ai's $1.89/hr offer for the h100-80gb-pcie as a competitive option for their high-performance computing needs.
