SAN FRANCISCO, JUNE 13, 2026 — Vast.ai maintains its position as the cheapest provider for the H100-80GB-SXM, pricing it at $2.29/hr. 

Engineers training large models continue to face high costs, and the current prices reflect the ongoing competition among cloud providers to offer the best value for high-end GPUs. 

According to PriceGPU data, the H100-80GB-SXM is currently priced at $2.29/hr on Vast.ai, while the H100-80GB-PCIE is available for $1.89/hr, also on Vast.ai, making it a more affordable option for engineers who do not require the SXM form factor. 

The lack of significant price changes since the last scrape indicates a stable market, which allows engineers to plan their budgets with more confidence, knowing that prices are unlikely to fluctuate wildly in the short term. 

With prices holding steady, an engineer looking to rent a high-end GPU today can consider the A100-80GB-SXM on Fluidstack for $1.85/hr or the H100-80GB-PCIE on Vast.ai for $1.89/hr, depending on their specific needs and budget constraints.
