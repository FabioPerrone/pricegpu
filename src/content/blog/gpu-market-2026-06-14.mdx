SAN FRANCISCO, JUNE 14, 2026 — Vast.ai maintains its lead in the H100 market with a price of $2.29/hr for the h100-80gb-sxm model.

The stability of GPU cloud rental prices is a welcome respite for machine learning engineers, who can now budget with greater certainty as they plan their compute resources. 

Engineers are likely to take advantage of the current prices to train and deploy their models, especially with the h100-80gb-pcie model available for $1.89/hr, also on Vast.ai, which offers a more affordable option for those who don't require the sxm form factor. 

According to PriceGPU data, the a100-80gb-sxm model is currently priced at $1.85/hr on Fluidstack, while the rtx-4090 is available for $0.24/hr on Salad, and the l40s for $0.99/hr on Fluidstack. 

PriceGPU tracking shows that prices have remained flat since the last scrape, which means engineers can plan their budgets without worrying about sudden price spikes, allowing them to allocate their resources more efficiently. 

With the current prices holding steady, an engineer looking to rent a GPU today can consider the rtx-3090 on Salad for $0.13/hr as a cost-effective option for inference workloads or less demanding tasks.
